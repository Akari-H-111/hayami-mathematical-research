\section{The mathematical correction}
The completed source has five rank-loss labels, including two endpoint
corners omitted in v12. Actual derivative determinants establish the
applicable smooth cross-cap recognition hypotheses. The polar observation
map has genuine fold coordinates, independently of its incomplete Taylor
remainder. Actual small-circle winding and exponential-cover lifting establish
square-root sign change. The real link, weighted orbit and actual curvature
exceptional cusp are distinct constructions.

The revised Mellin result fixes the measure and inner-product convention and
includes the endpoint term. On $L^2(dx)$ its formal adjoint parameter is
$1-\sigma$; on $L^2(x^{2\beta-1}dx)$ it is $2\beta-\sigma$.
The logarithmic unitary gives the actual half-line closure and adjoint
domains $H^1_0$ and $H^1$, with deficiency indices $(1,0)$.
The Gram entries are actual integrals. Every finite distinct frequency family
is independent for every $T>0$; separation bounds give conditioning and
log-determinant estimates.

Capacity is proved by logarithmic cutoffs at all five labels. A different,
weighted radial Hardy estimate controls $L^2$ mass near each singularity by
$O(\varepsilon^2)$ times the form norm. Ordinary Rellich compactness on the
regular complement then proves actual compact form embedding and a positive
fixed-Dirichlet gap, including both endpoint corners.

The old compact-core minimum leaves infinitely many outer-boundary deficiency
and harmonic modes. A collar extension followed by the fixed-Dirichlet
resolvent injects arbitrary smooth regular-boundary data into those spaces.
Thus the old $(2,2)$ and exhaustive $U(2)$ classification fail as defined.
The actual closed positive minimum still has a Krein extension and discrete
reduced positive spectrum; its kernel is infinite-dimensional. The applicable
abstract theorem gives the weak buckling correspondence on its actual domain.
A finite point restriction is a different, conditional construction requiring
graph-continuous surjective point traces.

\section{The remaining analytic construction}
The actual fixed-Dirichlet point restriction needs bounded graph traces
$\tau:\operatorname{Dom}H_F\to\C^2$ and a dense kernel. A two-sided
Green parametrix must prove continuous resolvent correction for the displayed
local $L^p$ remainder, identify exhaustive maximal-domain coefficients and
control overlap errors in specified graph spaces.

For the Dirac problem, specify the spin/Pin convention, singular-cut domains,
flat phase-line fiber identification and a Clifford-compatible unitary cut
map. A spin Pin lift alone supplies no map of those separate phase fibers.
The regular-bank scalar Green calculation is conditional on these data.
Under dilation the normal cutoff commutator has weight zero; shrinking the
patch alone gives no small Neumann-series remainder.

The chosen complex $A_3$ completion has explicit compactification and divisor,
so external Milnor, Gysin and mixed-Hodge theorems have stated input objects.
Normalization support/recollement and Clifford/Pin results remain external.
These results, compactness and operator-domain proofs are not formalized by
the present inventory. The current checkpoint is partial coverage; its
manifest or successful replay does not make it full-paper Lean coverage.

hash-locked PDF and rejects missing or duplicate named entries.
