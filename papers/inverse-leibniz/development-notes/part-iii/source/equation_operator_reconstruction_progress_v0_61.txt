v0.61 Equation-Operator Reconstruction Theorem

GOAL:
Remove separately declared A_eq.

INPUT:
  finite filtered relation resolution P_*
  canonical chain-operator family
    G_can={S_arity, Delta_1,...,Delta_r}
  distinguished relation seeds R0

CHAIN DESCENT:
Every G in G_can satisfies dG=Gd, hence preserves im d1 and descends to
  Gbar in End(H0(P_*)).

RECONSTRUCTED EFFECTIVE ALGEBRA:
  A_eq^eff = k<Gbar : G in G_can> subset End(H0(P_*))

FREE-ALGEBRA FORM:
  ev_P:k<X_G> -> End(H0)
  A_eq^eff = im ev_P ~= k<X_G>/ker ev_P.
Thus operator relations are discovered from the presentation rather
than postulated.

MINIMALITY:
A_eq^eff is the unique smallest unital subalgebra containing all
effective canonical operators.

NATURALITY:
If Phi:P->P' is a filtered chain comparison inducing H0 iso and
  Phi G - G' Phi = dH_G + H_G d,
then H0(Phi) conjugates A_eq^eff(P) onto A_eq^eff(P').

RESOLUTION INDEPENDENCE:
Raw chain actions can differ arbitrarily on contractible summands.
Effective H0 action and A_eq^eff remain unchanged.

FILTERED TRIANGULARITY:

positive-filtration canonical operators is nilpotent.
