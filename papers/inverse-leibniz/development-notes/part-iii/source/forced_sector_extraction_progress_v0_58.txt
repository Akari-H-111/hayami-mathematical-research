v0.58 Forced-Sector Extraction Theorem

INPUT:
  finite data state (M,T)
  landing Lambda:M->R
  source D:R->F
  normalized N subset F
  quotient nu:F->F/N

NORMALIZED TEST:
  Gamma=nu D Lambda

STATIC FORCED SECTOR:
  K=D^{-1}(N) cap im Lambda
  K ~= ker Gamma / ker Lambda
  dim K = rank Lambda - rank Gamma

LANDING CLOSURE:
  ker Lambda T-invariant
  equivalent rank[Lambda;Lambda T]=rank Lambda
  then recurrent A is induced by T on M/ker Lambda.

NORMALIZED CLOSURE:
  A(K) subset K
  iff ker Gamma T-invariant
  iff rank[Gamma;Gamma T]=rank Gamma.

OPERATOR:
  A|K is conjugate to induced T on ker Gamma / ker Lambda.

NON-INVARIANT CASE:

        = ker O_N
  O_N=[Gamma;Gamma T;...;Gamma T^(m-1)]
  K_inf ~= M_inf/ker Lambda
  dim K_inf=rank Lambda-rank O_N.

FORCING BRIDGE:
  Lambda=I P_F(T)
  Gamma=nu D I P_F(T)
So forced sector comes directly from forcing matrices.

  p_forced=d_Lambda/d_Gamma.
