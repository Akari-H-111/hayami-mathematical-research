v0.60 Syzygy-to-Constraint Propagator Theorem

KEY CORRECTION:
Bare vector-space syzygies do NOT determine propagators.
Need an equation-operator algebra A_eq acting on the relation
presentation before contraction.

SETUP:
  A_eq canonical source-operation algebra from deformation presentation
  P1 --d1--> P0 --eps--> F_rel -> 0
  exact in category of left A_eq-modules

SYZYGY DESCENT:
  im d1 is automatically A_eq-stable.
  M_rel=P0/im d1=H0(P_*) inherits canonical A_eq action:
    rho_rel(a)[p]=[ap].

PROPAGATORS:
Transport through H0 ~= F_rel:
  C(a)=epsbar rho_rel(a) epsbar^{-1}.
For computational generators u_i:
  C_i=C(u_i).

CANONICAL CONSTRAINT ALGEBRA:
  C_syz=im(C:A_eq->End(F_rel))
       ~= A_eq/Ann(M_rel).

INVARIANCE:
- generator choice changes displayed tuple, not C_syz
- source basis changes matrices by similarity
- syzygy basis change does nothing
- A_eq-linear comparison of resolutions intertwines H0 actions
- different A_eq-linear free resolutions of same relation module give
  same action on H0

REFINEMENT OF v0.59:
No need to extend C_i to all ambient F.
The canonical action lives on F_rel; ambient extensions are noncanonical
and irrelevant.

v0.59 REWRITTEN:

      = epsbar(A_eq . epsbar^{-1}(im R0)).
