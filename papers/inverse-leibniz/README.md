Shared reproducibility materials have [collection DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22663942](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22663942).
Cite the individual paper for its mathematics and the collection when using its
shared materials. See the [verification guide](../../verification/README.md).

Paper I's certificates use a fixed contraction and finite-order data. The
independent-transfer and feedback-matrix constructions are new constructions,
not recovered historical matrices. Paper II's naturality requires the stated
resonance-faithful marking; it is not bare quasi-isomorphism invariance. Paper
III's marks and maps are supplied data, not automatic consequences of a bare
filtration. The general filtered-complex/Diophantine bridge is a separate problem.
