This independent companion maps every asserted mathematical result of the
corrected v5 article to actual Lean statements, their hypotheses, and exact
replay. The 224 public named theorems of this project are audited separately
from the 258 reused Stokes v5 dependency theorems. Lean and Mathlib are

\section{Actual objects and retained domains}
The surface is the actual function
\[
 S(t,u)=(\cos t+2u(1-\cos t),(1-u)\sin t,
 u\sqrt{\cos t(2-\cos t)}).
\]
The full physical labels satisfy $|t|\leq\pi/2$ and $0\leq u\leq1$.
Angular derivative formulas use $Q^2=\cos t(2-\cos t)>0$.
At each endpoint use the actual smooth map
\[
 c_r=1-\sqrt{1-r^2},\quad s_r=\varepsilon\sqrt{1-c_r^2},\quad
 S_\varepsilon(r,u)=(c_r+2u(1-c_r),(1-u)s_r,ur),
\]
where $\varepsilon=\pm1$ and physically $0\leq r<1/2$.
Smooth extensions use $|r|<1$; a negative extension parameter is not
silently identified with the physical square root $Q$.

A genuine global parameter is
\[
 c_g=(1-v^2)^2,\quad
 s_g=v\sqrt{(2-v^2)(1+(1-v^2)^2)},\quad
 q_g=(1-v^2)\sqrt{2-c_g},
\]
for $v\in[-1,1]$, with $t_g=2\arcsin(v\sqrt{1-v^2/2})$.
The Lean product of two interval manifolds with boundary gives the actual
manifold with corners $[-1,1]\times[0,1]$. The global map is smooth in
that model, $t_g$ gives a homeomorphism to the original labels, and the
actual angular and boundary transitions have smooth local inverses.
Thus the $Q=0$ edges are retained. The five rank losses count source
labels; they do not classify distinct image points.

Curvature uses the Euclidean dot product, actual first and second
Fr\'echet derivatives, the normalized cross product, and the ratio of
the two fundamental-form determinants. It is not a definition by the
desired rational formula. The second-order chain rule proves consistency
on the actual atlas overlaps $(r,u)\mapsto(T(r),u)$ with $T'\ne0$.
It does not assert a general arbitrary-coordinate curvature theorem.

The observation is the actual complex field $F=N+iM$ on $Q>0$, where
\[
 N=(1-c)(1-c-2u),\qquad M=\frac{c^2(2-c)-u(1-c+c^2)}Q.
\]
It transforms as a source-area density and is not declared a global
chart-independent scalar field. The physical interior used for local
indices is $(-\pi/2,\pi/2)\times(0,1)$.

\section{Genuine winding and the lateral dipole}
For a closed nonzero complex loop, divide by its initial value and lift
it through the complex exponential with initial logarithm zero. Winding
is the imaginary endpoint divided by $2\pi$. The formal construction
proves integer winding, initial-logarithm independence and free closed-loop
homotopy invariance. It then proves that the image of a positive source
circle under an invertible real-linear map has winding equal to its
orientation sign, through a nonvanishing ellipse homotopy.

For an actual nondegenerate zero, differentiability gives
$F(p+h)=Lh+o(\lVert h\rVert)$. An inverse-operator bound makes the
homotopy from the derivative circle to every sufficiently small actual
observation circle nonzero. Radius bounds keep all those source circles
inside the physical interior. Applying this theorem gives actual winding
$-1$ and $+1$ at the positive and negative lateral zeros. Their local
index is defined by this all-small-radius property and is proved unique;
it is not defined as a determinant sign. The polar point $(0,1)$ has no
interior index: every positive source circle crosses $u>1$.

The classical planar-index convention is background. No unproved
local-degree theorem or custom axiom fills this formalization. A separate
general homological construction of Brouwer degree is not claimed.

\section{Reproducible verification and trust boundary}
\texttt{verify\_lean.py} builds the entire project, checks the exhaustive
status inventory, prints every public theorem's axioms, and rejects missing
or duplicated audit entries. Only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice},
and \texttt{Quot.sound} are allowed. The source scan rejects proof holes,
custom axioms, unsafe proofs and native-decision shortcuts. Audited theorem
dependencies include the definitions and private lemmas used in their
proofs. The pinned Stokes project is separately audited and is not counted
as new Ruled results.

The independent exact verifier differentiates actual maps and replays
circle identities, rank elimination, tangent limits, curvature, coordinate
transport, polynomial signs and root isolation, and both recurrence failure
mechanisms. SymPy is pinned to 1.14.0. Python optimization is forbidden
because replay uses assertions. Finite figure samples only illustrate
formula-derived curves; they do not prove geometric or Fourier claims.

The portable candidate's \texttt{verify.py} checks every manifest entry,
historical authority, distinct publication identities, both Lean inventories,
exact calculations and source/native/PDF bindings. It rebuilds both PDFs in
isolation with three passes and compares their full text and every accepted
page rendering. In-place and extracted replays must both pass. Same-host
reuse of pinned Mathlib packages and identical-source Stokes cache is
disclosed in the receipt; it is not an independent-host reproduction.

The native desktop compiler verifies each final standalone source. It
returns diagnostics, so PDF export and all-page visual QA are separate
checks. SHA-256 binds sources, exports, inspected pages and acceptance
records. Earlier unsuccessful proof or layout attempts are retained as
rejected evidence, never promoted to passes.
