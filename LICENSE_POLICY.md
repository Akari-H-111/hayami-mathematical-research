# License policy decision

Approved mixed-license policy:

- Lean and Python source: Apache License 2.0.
- Manuscripts, PDFs, original figures, and project documentation: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
- `archive/` and `releases/archive/`: no new license grant until the rights and provenance of each recovered or supplied item have been checked.

This split gives the executable formalization an explicit software and patent license while keeping the scholarly material reusable with attribution. Zenodo supports mixed-license uploads.

Status: **APPROVED BY THE AUTHOR on 2026-09-12**.

The policy applies to the working repository and the new v0.02 companion
package. It does not rewrite the sealed v0.01 ZIP: its existing
`LICENSE_STATUS.md` and SHA-256 are part of the immutable historical record.
