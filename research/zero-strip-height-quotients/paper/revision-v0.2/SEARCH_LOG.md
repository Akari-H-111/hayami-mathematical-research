## Texts opened and portions checked

1. Ford--Zaharescu, [arXiv:math/0405459v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0405459),
   dated 2004-09-30: pp. 1--7 and bibliography p. 13.  Checked Hlawka/Fujii
   attribution, all-zero multiplicity convention, secondary measure, Theorem
   1/Corollary 2, uniform Landau estimate, and Selberg density lemma.
2. Ford--Soundararajan--Zaharescu,
   [arXiv:0805.2745v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.2745), dated 2009-01-10:
   pp. 1--3 and pp. 14--15.  Checked its explicit interval conjecture,
   discrepancy statement, and extension context.
3. Bui--Conrey--Young, [arXiv:1002.4127v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1002.4127),
   dated 2011-03-11: p. 1.  Checked the definitions and Theorem 1.1.
4. Burnol, [arXiv:1106.4749](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1106.4749), submitted
   2011-06-23: pp. 1--2 and pp. 10--12.  Checked the weaker Hamburger
   hypotheses and its arithmetic-progression support discussion.
5. Nakamura, [Jxiv preprint v2](https://jxiv.jst.go.jp/index.php/jxiv/preprint/download/620/2150/1971),
   2024-02-16: pp. 2--5, pp. 16--17.  Checked the Koshliakov/Knopp discussion,
   ordinary-Dirichlet-series boundary, main constructions, and RvM analogue.
6. Nakamura, [arXiv:1910.09837v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.09837), dated
   2021-07-14: pp. 1--4.  Checked the quadrilateral family and its relation to
   the Hamburger/Hecke/Knopp functional equation.
7. Dixit--Gupta, [arXiv:2108.00810v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.00810),
   dated 2021-08-02: pp. 3--6.  Checked Hamburger hypotheses and the
   Koshliakov pair.

## Retrieval failures / exclusions

- The direct Fujii 2002 repository PDF returned HTTP 429.  I did not infer
  theorem details from the unavailable full text; FZ and FSZ state the needed
  bound and were read directly.
- The supplied Kaczorowski--Molteni--Perelli author-hosted PDF returned an
  internal retrieval error.  It is listed as unavailable rather than newly
  inspected.
- Knopp's paper was located bibliographically at
  [NUMDAM/EuDML](https://geodesic.mathdoc.fr/item/IM_1994__117_1_144219/),
  but its full text was not available through the search path.
- A search result titled *The hamburger theorem* was opened and found to be a
  combinatorial-geometry paper about the ham-sandwich theorem, not Hamburger's
  theorem for zeta.  It was discarded.
- Searches returning “Mobius-function” (arithmetic `mu`) papers were discarded;
  they are not predecessors of a Mobius-band quotient.

## Coverage boundary

This log covers exact terms around sparse/prescribed zero products,
zero-density perturbations, quotient/pushforward geometry, mod-one zero
statistics, and Hamburger-type nonuniqueness.  It does not search all work on
RH, quantum chaos, every entire-function perturbation theorem, or every
converse theorem.  Therefore “not located” never means “does not exist.”
