# Final novelty verdict and supervisor corrections

Date: 2026-10-18. Read this together with NOVELTY_AUDIT.md and SEARCH_LOG.md.
The latter two preserve the delegated CLI's first-pass investigation. This
verdict includes primary sources independently found by the supervisor after
that pass; it controls the final manuscript's contribution language.

## Verdict

The audit is complete within its documented searches and inspected source
set. It does not confirm a new independent number-theoretic theorem or
worldwide priority. The appropriate genre is a worked mathematical synthesis
and explicit obstruction example. The named arithmetic results are known;
the geometric and packing conclusions are elementary corollaries; prescribed
symmetric zero insertion and functional-equation nonuniqueness have close
prior work. The exact dyadic order-zero example with the stated quantitative
transfer list was not located as a single prior statement, but its deductions
are routine and absence from this search is not evidence of first discovery.

## Material addition to the first CLI audit

P. M. Gauthier and X. Xarles, *Perturbations of L-functions with or without
non-trivial zeros off the critical line*, arXiv:0911.5135v1, 26 November 2009:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0911.5135 . Directly inspected Introduction pp. 1-2,
Proposition 1 pp. 6-7, Theorem 2 and proof pp. 7-8, and the start of Section 4.
Proposition 1 constructs an entire symmetric multiplier with a prescribed
symmetric zero set and approximation/interpolation. Theorem 2 retains the
old zero divisor off the prescribed set, poles and chosen Laurent data,
while imposing additional zeros and strong approximation on a large closed
set. Its symmetric prescribed set is disjoint from the retained divisor.

This is a close predecessor of D8/D9 and must be explicitly cited. The mere
existence of a multiplier adding off-critical zeros while retaining the
functional equation and old zeros is already prior art, beyond the generic
Hamburger discussion. The inspected theorem does not supply our specific
dyadic product, order-zero/tail estimates or the exact listed statistic
formulas as a printed package. We do not infer those constraints from its
abstract approximation theorem. Conversely, this difference does not make
our elementary transfer deductions a verified new analytic theorem.

The original earlier work P. M. Gauthier and E. S. Zeron, *Small Perturbations
of the Riemann Zeta Function and their Zeros*, Comput. Methods Funct. Theory
4 (2004), 143-150, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF03321061, was checked at the
publisher abstract/metadata level. Full publisher text was subscription-
limited; exact theorem comparisons above rely on the accessible 2009 paper.
The publisher's online posting date 2013 does not change the 2004 issue date.

## Corrections to first-pass source details

   (III)*, Comment. Math. Univ. St. Pauli 42 (1993), 161-187. Reference [4],
   Part IV, 43 (1994), 217-244, is used elsewhere for a Bernoulli-test result.
   The first-pass audit's attribution of the discrepancy reference to Part IV
   is corrected here. FZ/FSZ were directly checked; no stronger rate is used.
2. The KMP author is A. Perelli, not G. Perelli. Its author-hosted Introduction
   was directly read by the supervisor in the preceding cycle and rechecked
   here; a failure in the delegated retrieval does not invalidate that source.
3. Burnol supplies converse/rigidity context, not a zero-insertion theorem.
   The directly inspected Theorem 1 and Corollaries 1-2 are on PDF pp. 2-3.
4. The final D6 statement concerns closure of all critical-line ordinates;
   simple zeros supply its lower bound. No selected-subsequence
   equidistribution or density is proved.

## Search coverage and exclusions

SEARCH_LOG.md records the delegated exact queries; SUPERVISOR_LEADS.md records
additional primary leads and query families. Search terms cover mod-one
statistics, quotient/pushforward measures, sparse or prescribed zero
products, density-zero perturbations, and Hamburger-type rigidity. The
mathematical comparisons use read primary statements rather than snippets.

A 2025 preprint by F. O. Souto, *Where Geometry Meets Number Theory: A
Constructive Framework*, https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202512.2406,
v2 posted 31 December 2025, was inspected in Sections 1-3 as a terminology
lead. It postulates spectral/topological structures and a different seam
identification, rather than proving the fixed-height empirical-measure
theorem here. Its claims are not used as established analytic inputs. Merely
using Mobius language is neither an exact theorem match nor a priority basis.
Unrelated Mobius-function, quantum-cylinder and unsupported RH-proof search
results were excluded from proof dependencies.

External human refereeing, complete access to every historical text and an
exhaustive priority certification were not performed. The actionable outcome
is settled: v0.2 must present a synthesis/worked example, not a claimed new
RH criterion or independently novel zero-distribution theorem.
