# Additional primary leads inspected by the supervisor

Gauthier-Xarles, arXiv:0911.5135v1 (26 Nov 2009), Proposition 1 pp. 6-7 and
Theorem 2 pp. 7-8. https://arxiv.org/pdf/0911.5135 . This is a much closer
prior result than general Hamburger context: prescribed symmetric zeros,
unchanged old divisor away from them, same poles/principal parts and very
strong approximation. Do not claim our insertion idea is new. Our explicit
order-zero dyadic product has a count/tail/growth package that must be
compared separately, without asserting that general approximation gives
the exact same growth constraints automatically.

Gauthier-Zeron, Comput. Methods Funct. Theory 4 (2004), 143-150,
https://doi.org/10.1007/BF03321061 . Publisher abstract and bibliography were
inspected; publisher full text is subscription-limited. Its 2013 web posting
date must not replace the 2004 issue date.

Burnol, arXiv:1106.4749, Theorem 1/Corollaries 1-2 pp. 2-3. Meromorphic
Dirichlet-series converse extensions; not a prescribed-zero construction.
