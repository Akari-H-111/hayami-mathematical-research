# Height quotients of the Riemann zero divisor

Status: substantive local thematic manuscript v0.1, 18 October 2026.
This directory is separate from the existing ten-paper catalogue. Nothing
has been committed, pushed or published by this task.

Read the [nine-page paper](output/pdf/height-quotients-v0.1.pdf), with its
[authoritative TeX source](manuscript/main.tex). The earlier
MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT.md, RESEARCH_CORE.md and DIRECTOR_DERIVATIONS.md retain the
research progression; the final TeX controls the delivered claims.

The connected question is what a fixed-height cylinder or Mobius observation
can identify about the Riemann zero divisor. The paper proves a quantitative
central-circle probability limit from classical unconditional inputs, excludes
full and eventual support periods, and gives a simple-critical-line residue
bound. It then constructs an infinite sparse off-critical perturbation that
preserves the named probability and smooth secondary limits, counting and
Landau asymptotic classes, and functional equation/pole normalization. An
ordinary absolutely convergent Dirichlet-series condition rejects this model.
This is a synthesis and obstruction study, not a proof or counterexample to RH.

## Research boundary and next questions

No result excludes repeated individual gaps or approximate recurrence. The
critical-line-only sequence is not proved equidistributed here. Smooth
secondary transfer does not settle the interval conjecture. The product
model does not have ordinary Dirichlet-series or claimed Euler-product
structure, and no exact explicit formula is preserved.

Useful next work is to identify exact arithmetic tests that distinguish the
sparse model, or quantify what tests depending on truncation height can detect
it. A broader specialist overlap review is appropriate before submission.
