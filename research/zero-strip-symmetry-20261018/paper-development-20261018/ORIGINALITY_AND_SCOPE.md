# Originality, overlap, and scope assessment

The arithmetic input is classical. Hlawka's all-zero equidistribution and the
Ford--Zaharescu smooth secondary measures are prior results; the positive
proportion of simple critical-line zeros is prior work of Bui--Conrey--Young.
They are not claimed as new.

The connected contribution is a quotient-focused synthesis with the following
derived deductions.

1. The support-period obstruction is stronger than the parent
   multiplicity-divisor argument. It identifies the missing ingredient:
   equidistribution plus compact-slab local finiteness. It handles eventual
   periods and glides and proves distinct-residue density, not merely weighted
   occurrence.
2. The compact quotient theorem separates raw pushforward (non-Radon),
   normalized empirical measures (central Haar), and a descended invariant
   divisor (impossible). The Mobius seam is handled by a continuous-test proof.
3. The explicit sparse entire factor gives an infinite controlled blindness
   result: these quotient limits and the proved smooth secondary statistic
   survive while infinitely many off-line zeros are inserted.

The residue lower bound is an elementary corollary of BCY's exact simple-zero
definition, not a number-theoretic breakthrough. The product lemma is standard
Weierstrass-product analysis. Any novelty claim should be limited to the
assembled application to these quotient diagnostics.

The auxiliary X has no Euler product or zeta interpretation; weak convergence
on the center circle permits a zero-density off-line exceptional set; and no
interval secondary asymptotic is asserted. The focused review in LITERATURE_POSITION.md identifies known overlap and
related non-Dirichlet constructions. It does not establish priority for the
exact sparse-perturbation package. A broader specialist overlap review remains
appropriate before publication; no priority claim is made.
