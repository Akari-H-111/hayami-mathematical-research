## Corrections and limits of the raw reviews

The reports are retained unedited as independent evidence. These points control
how their suggestions should be used:

- **Page geometry:** there are twelve rotated figure sheets in four blocks and
  eight changes of displayed orientation. DeepSeek's initial twelve-change
  wording confuses rotated sheets with transitions; its final report correctly
  enumerates four blocks. Graphic-box area is not ink coverage, and extracted
  word counts are not scientific merit or prose-length measurements.
- **Cross-references:** Figure 5 is included in the prose range Figures 1–5;
  DeepSeek's table saying it is unreferenced is inaccurate. Figure 10 is not
  referenced in the running text; its table saying it is referenced contradicts
  its own correctly stated orphan-figure finding. Figures 8–12 do need references.
- **Proof continuity:** the current named theorem statements and their proofs
  are not split by the plate blocks. The interruptions separate related results,
  remarks and definitions. Adopt the latter criticism rather than claiming a
  statement/proof split that is not present.
- **Rank loss:** in the stated interior domain, u=(1-c)/2 is a necessary
  condition obtained from one cross-product component. Rank loss also requires
  P(c)=0. The displayed remaining component has a nonzero prefactor, so the
  combined conditions give the interior golden point. Do not use the proposed
  caption “the rank-loss locus is u=(1-c)/2” for the entire line. Add an explicit
  iff sentence for clarity; this is not evidence of a false surface theorem.
- **Citations:** the inversion law explicitly cites Duke Proposition 2. The local
  geometric source is cited in the text as reference [5]. Improvements needed
  are placement/precise locators and stable access, not invention of absent
  attributions. A product-formula locator can be added. The number of references
  alone is not a scholarly-quality test.
- **Reproducibility scope:** the checkers, plotted data and rebuildable source
  already exist in the delivered bundle. They were intentionally withheld from
  the isolated first-read packet. The reviewers' inability to replay them does
  not undo the earlier recorded checks. The PDF should point readers to them.
- **Typography evidence:** use the sampled PDF transform measurement for the
  small-label diagnosis. DeepSeek's XML font summary omits some inherited font
  entries and can include math scripts or clipped strings; its smallest-run
  number is not a verified minimum ordinary-label size. The actual captions use
  the normal approximately 10.9-point article font. Font thresholds are proposed
  review criteria, not universal journal regulations.
- **Color and appearance:** remove the unreliable global semantic color key.
  A grayscale or color-vision failure has not been independently demonstrated;
  test the new version rather than declaring a completed accessibility audit.
- **Author and venue:** retain exactly Akari Hayami (Jian-Yu Huang) and
  akari.h.1.9984@gmail.com. Personal email is not itself a formal defect. Do not
  invent an affiliation. A draft label is appropriate for the current initial
  draft; submission metadata depends on an actual venue. An isolated full-page
  figure can be justified by content, so a categorical ban is unnecessary.
