# Cold-review method and evidence

This is a solicited local AI cold review focused on mathematical-article form,
proportions and incoming graduate-reader experience. It is not external human
peer review or a new literature/priority audit.

Two fresh, separate CLI sessions received the same frozen PDF, nineteen page
renders, TeX source and auxiliary PDF text/geometry. Neither received the author
conversation, earlier reviews, assembly decisions, other worker's findings or
project board. Operational installed skills were permitted; manuscript history
was excluded. The first impression was written before inspecting the TeX.

The director checked the actual image-read calls and file-access order in the
CLI event/session records: both read all nineteen images and wrote FIRST_READ.md
before opening main.tex. Stage-two source/geometry inspection then supported
specific layout diagnoses. The reviews' own LIMITS.md files record their actual
checks. The director verified that all supplied page renders match the completed
draft's hash-bound visual-QA record; no figure or manuscript was revised.

The physical PDF has nineteen Letter media boxes. Twelve display in landscape,
in blocks of five, two, three and two; seven display in portrait. There are eight
changes between displayed orientations. Page-count proportions describe document
allocation, not scientific merit or a journal requirement. Extracted word counts
include equations and clipped source text and are not exposition measurements.

Raw worker reports remain unedited. The director's consolidation distinguishes
consensus, independent judgment, measured facts and corrections. A proposed
redesign is a recommendation; no revised article is delivered by this review.
All prior bound manuscript/figure/checkpoint files and frozen review inputs are
checked against their saved hashes. CLI event logs, temporary image crops and
runtime diagnostics are retained locally under ignored logs or worker temporary
files; durable review outputs are the Markdown reports and provenance records.
